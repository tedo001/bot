Methodology

imitation learning, closed loop
  1. 1

    Build the work cell in simulation

    A Yaskawa Motoman GP7 built from the vendor's CAD meshes and kinematics runs on MuJoCo or PyBullet with real physics, inverse kinematics and grasping. One calibrated camera model is shared by the renderer and perception.

  2. 2

    See and understand the scene

    RT-DETR finds objects and people, RF-DETR segments them and PaddleOCR reads their labels. Labels are matched to detections and lifted to 3D, so the instruction's words map straight to positions.

  3. 3

    Generate expert demonstrations

    A rule-based planner is told each task directly and runs thousands of episodes: random layouts, varied phrasing, injected noise so the student learns to recover, and requests for absent objects whose right answer is to do nothing.

  4. 4

    Learn the policy by imitation

    A small language-conditioned transformer (2 layers, d = 96) learns from those demonstrations to point at the source and destination objects and output the next 8 actions and the probability the task is done.

  5. 5

    Evaluate in closed loop

    The trained model drives the arm on new layouts and unseen phrasings in both simulators, through the full camera → perception → policy path, and its scores are saved with the checkpoint.

  6. 6

    Run it safely

    The policy re-plans every 2 ticks of a 20 Hz loop and stops after two done predictions. A single controller enforces E-STOP, person-in-view hold, watchdog and rate limits; every message is schema-validated.
